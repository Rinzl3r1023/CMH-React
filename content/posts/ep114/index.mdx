#### **Bio**

Morgan is the Director of Execution and Evolution at JBarrows Sales Training, focusing on delivering to sales development teams to enhance their skill sets and performance.

In his previous role, he was the Sales Development Manager at Terminus where he managed a team of 13 reps to help B2B Marketers do Account-Based Marketing at scale. In addition, he is the host of The SDR Chronicles, which is a YouTube channel specifically geared for SDRs that provides motivation, advice, and tactics. His work has been featured in Forbes, Sales Hacker Hubspot Blog and Harvard Business Review.

#### **Episode Synopsis**

In this episode, Morgan and I talk about what it requires to build a successful sales team and not just be a successful sales person.

We discuss how you can standout in your sales efforts now that we've become such a saturated market with digital communication and where people are making the biggest mistakes in their sales efforts.

We wrap up by chatting about how to manage both your personal goals with your professional aspirations and do so without having to make sacrifices.
