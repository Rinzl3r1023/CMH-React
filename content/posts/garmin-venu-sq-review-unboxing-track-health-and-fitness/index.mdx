## **Garmin Venu SQ PROs**

## **Garmin App**

If you’ve already been using a Garmin product, it still uses the Garmin Connect app.

And [Garmin Venu SQ](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/707174) App is way more seamless with the connectivity aspect. And the good thing is you’ve already got the same interface.

If you’re familiar with it, it’s very straightforward.

You can go through many customization options to determine what you want on your dash.

Overall, it still has the winning features.

## **High and low heart rate alerts**

[Garmin Venu SQ](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/707174) is excellent in nudging you when you’re a little stressed, and it’ll prompt you to do some breathing exercises.

Or when you’ve been sitting for a while, it’ll alert you that it’s time to move.

So that’s a phenomenal part of why it’s fantastic as a business owner’s tool or resource.
So I liked it. It’s the same thing in terms of the the the watch face itself. I love that it has that feel to it.

## **Screen Face**

The amazing screen feature is that you can the meaningful things add to the dash as your opening screen.

You can add where it has the total steps for the day, the body battery, and the actual time, or whatever is important for you to see.

So that was cool for me—super great features.

## **Watch Face**

The watch face is pretty much the same as the Vivo Smart, which is excellent but more extensive.

And many of the complaints about the Vivo Smart were like the screens very small.

So this has a lot of visual representations, but it’s fundamental. It also doesn’t overwhelm you.

## **Activities Button**

So there are only two buttons.

There’s a button to go back to the menu. And there’s a button to look at different activities.

It has many activities you can add, like running, see treadmill, an indoor track, biking, swimming, golf, skiing, etc.

Maybe not as many as the others, but you’ll have the things that regular people would do. So I think you’re getting all of that there.

## **Swapping Bands**

One of my issues with Vivosmart was that it is what it is.

But with [Garmin Venu SQ](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/707174), it’s great that we can swap the bands with this watch.

And you can get many color options. So I think that’s cool.

## **BIGGEST PRO – Long-Lasting Battery**

The Battery legitimately lasts forever in a day.

I’ve gone a whole week where I go on a trip or don’t have to worry about bringing the charger with me.

It does take longer to charge because the Battery does last longer. But you’re getting a longer length of Battery on this device. And I think that is tremendous.

## **GARMIN VENU SQ CONs**

## **Steps Delayed Documentation**

I feel [Garmin Venu SQ](https://www.garmin.com/en-US/p/707174) is a little delayed documenting the steps for some reason.

And I haven’t entirely figured out why that is the case.

In real-time tracking, Vivosmart was faster in documenting steps.

## **OVER TO YOU**

Overall, I have to say. Garmin Venu SQ is my favorite.

I’m going to wear this one for a long time.

I’ve tried [Whoop and Halo](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=XREJ6ngSG_s), the Garmin Vivosmart, and a lot of these things, and I honestly think this one is at the top of my list.

It has a clean aesthetic like the Apple Watch and has fantastic features. It’s super simple, so it’s not going to overwhelm people. But it has the main functionality that, as a business owner, you are looking for.

As far as business owners are concerned, you cannot go wrong with the Garmin Venu SQ by measuring those vital measurements for operating at your best for your business.
