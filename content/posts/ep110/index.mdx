### **Bio**

Named by The Huffington Post as one of the “Top 50 Must-Follow Women Entrepreneurs,” Heather Havenwood is regarded as a top authority on digital marketing and online business sales strategies.

Marketing and building her first online business in 1999, she has played an active role in the digital marketing world since before most even had a home computer! In 2006 she started, developed and grew an online publishing company from ground zero to over $1 million in sales in less than 12 months. In 2015, she did it again, launching a local Austin weight loss organization and an online supplement store brand, going from zero to 1.5 in 18 months.

Now, Heather Ann helps businesses and entrepreneurs experience outstanding growth by coaching them in the hottest marketing techniques, such as the future of chatbots, digital measurement marketing, and online social behaviors analysis.
Heather Ann is also an outspoken advocate for women entrepreneurship and authored the best selling book, SexyBoss™, to educate women how to take control of their business results and their personal confidence in themselves.

### **Interview Synopsis**

In this episode, Heather Ann and I talk about her incredible journey from achieving early success as an entrepreneur to then finding herself with a bankruptcy attorney and having to live with a significant other to sustain her needs only to end up back on top of the mountaintop...scaling/selling business ventures.

We talk about some of the hottest digital marketing strategies to talk your idea or business venture to the next level. Which platforms should you focus on? What strategies will work and who should you be listening to for advice?

We wrap up talking about the future of the digital marketing world as well as Heather Ann's other fascinating initiatives in empowering female entrepreneurs to talk control of their own financial futures.
