## **The College Moving Company Was Officially Born in February of 2012**


My brother and I had this noble idea that we could somehow make decent money and not have to flip hamburgers.

As college students, we really wanted to focus our efforts on school and not spending the majority of our free time making minimum wage.

I already had a long history of entrepreneurial side hustles under my belt.

In fact, one summer while I was in high school, a friend of mine and I launched a handyman type side gig.

In a matter of 3 months we were able to generate somewhere in the ballpark of $12,000.

Well, $6,000 of that came from one job request.

An elderly couple had a really bad experience with a moving company some 20 years prior pledged to never go that route again.

They wanted a different alternative to your typical blue-collar approach.

Growing up in an affluent area, most of my friends had an understanding of how important people's belongings are to them.

We also had one of the best school systems in the entire state.

Now, I'm not saying we were ALL a bunch of spoiled and smart rich kids.

In fact, most of my friends and I were on the lower end of that totem pole.

Somewhere in the upper-middle range.

So, our parents wanted us to "learn the value of a dollar."

And if I actually had a dollar for every time I heard my father say that I wouldn't need another dollar, I assure you (HA!).

So, this elderly couple saw one of our business cards pinned to the bulletin board at our local grocery store and decided to give us a call.

We visited the couple's home, realized what a daunting task it was and decided there was no way we could say no.

They respected the fact a couple of kids were spending their time in such enterprising fashion and enjoyed us as young men.

That experience, while then fast forwarded to my college apartment some 5 years later, really stuck with me.

You see, where we lacked in shear, brute force, we made up for with intelligence and basic laws of physics.

That elderly couple loved every minute of the experience with us so why couldn't we replicate that same concept again!?

Well, one day we see two petite college girls at our complex trying to move one of those massive sleeper sofas down the hall.

And, mind you, this hallway was like the tunnel of doom.

If it stretched an entire 8th of mile I wouldn't be surprised.

Struggling mightily, my brother and I decided to lend them a helping hand.

Not five minutes later and the sofa was secured and ready for transit.

The mother threw us a handsome tip and we went about our day.

Again, wheels are now churning even faster.

I told my brother we should go talk to the Lease Office Manager, whom we had a good relationship with.

And why she liked us I have no idea...

Evidently our unit, 320, had become notorious for our loud and obnoxious parties.

But either way, she wanted to support our efforts.

We had employees in 32 states, working with five of the seven players in our industry.

## **So, What Did This Magic System Look Like?**


Ohhhh, man...

How much time do you have?

Because this post could go on for days.

But here goes, I'm literally going to give you the blueprint...the secret, to scaling any service based business.

And remember, I don't normally do bold statements like that due to individualized needs.

But I believe in this wholeheartedly.

At least, in principle.

So, in other words, you can take this basic theories and apply it to your own business.

It may not look and feel or even function in the exact same manner but your light bulbs with be flashing at you with the high beams on blast.

And, for the sake of some kind of continuity, I'll try to keep this is some form of concise order.



## **Hiring:**


We've all been there, right?

Interviews and applications can largely be faked with easy questions that literally don't give you any valid information in making a good hire.

We knew this and opted to automate the entire process, removing nearly all touch points for one of our administrators.

The system graded their responses, passed them along to a pre-recorded video interview tool and if their score was high enough, we would then meet with the candidate.

The information they filled out was also automatically passed up to our Payroll/HR service provider in the event we triggered a response that shows we wanted to hire that candidate.

The system removed 50 minutes from the hiring process for our admins, per hire!



## **Training:**


All of our training materials were included in a video series where we could track their progress.

This training also graded them on a quiz to ensure they not only passed with a 90% or higher but also gave them explanations for why their wrong answers were indeed wrong.

Once training was completed, a notification was sent to both our admins and HR provider to complete the onboarding process and begin scheduling.



## **Levels and Rankings:**


You can't just throw people into the fire, you have to build a system to gradually introduce them to the work they'll be doing.

By quantifying these job functions based on level of difficulty and experience, we found a drastic reduction in our customer complaints and damages.

In fact, what we found was 40% of our issues were coming from our new employees that just didn't have the experience to do the job at a high level.

By giving them a path to succeed, we not only reduced many headaches but also maintained higher employee morale as our employees no longer felt overwhelmed when first hired.



## **Resources:**


Don't you hate when employees interupt your major work with basic questions?

It feels like you can never move the business forward because you spend the majority of your day telling people how to do their jobs.

Proper training material will certainly help but we also created a resources page on the back end of our website that was password protected.

This page was an encyclopedia of potential issues that may arise and video material on how to solve them.

We also recorded examples of other employees handling those situations so they got a real world perspective and not just theoretical.

Our field employees had a similar platform with an entirely different login portal.



## **Furniture Database:**


This was my Mona Lisa, for what it's worth.

We were able to get hyper-specific on the nature of what all furniture consists of.

By approaching an item based on it's attributes instead of the item itself, we could give instructions on how to approach items with those conditions.

For example, fine finished wood involves certain protocols, regardless of the item in nature.

So, a coffee table with fine finished wood is approached the exact same way a night stand with fine finished wood is approached.

Our field employees could select attributes from a database and corresponding instructions, plus video, were populated to give them guidance.

This could be pulled up on their company tablets in the field.



## **Field Paperwork:**


I hate paper.

I used to say that all the time at the office.

How can we eliminate more paper?

Boxes upon boxes of paperwork and nobody truly knows where anything is.

We eliminated 13 pages of field paperwork, per job, by digitizing the entire process.

This also gave us the ability to take and attach pictures, record customer signatures and prevent field employees from moving beyond where they were in the process.

If they left a required field blank, they would not be able to submit and move forward.

All of these submissions went into a database for our admins to reference.

We also built in an amazing review system as well as an automated responder for people customers that indicated problems on the job.

If they filled in scores with an average less than 80%, alerts were sent out to admins as well as the customer to get ahead of a potential bad situation.



## **Scheduling:**


You literally can't lose employees fast enough if you don't have good scheduling protocols in place.

For businesses where customers request the times they want to be serviced, we based our system solely on employee availability.

That way, there was no way we would ever be left short-handed.

This was a far removal from our competitors but the results were overwhelming and customer satisfaction went through the roof.

Our employees were happy and felt like they had much more predictability in their schedule.

We also used time slots to accommodate longer requests versus shorter requests.

This prevented work going beyond business hours and controlled overtime expenditures.



## **Pricing**


One of our other proud moments was when we developed an automated pricing system for both our installation and residential moving requests.

On residential requests, we found many "window shoppers."

These people took up an exhorbitant amount of time and really weren't ready to hop on the phone just yet.

We crafted a tool with weighted figures to give them a ballpark idea on price and found it was within 15% of accuracy from a phone quote.

On the commercial/installation side of the business, we could measure down to nine decimal points of accuracy and provide multiple six figure quotes in 20 minutes of less. We later discovered our nearest competitor took about 2-3 days.

As you can imagine, this gave us a major competitive advantage.



## **Field Service Supervision**


We began working on a system where our employees would attach webcams to their hats.

This system was no unlike what you would see police officers wearing.

Now, we never actually launched this initiative because of customer push back in having their personal items recorded and reviewed.

We believe the more technology becomes mainstream in our lives, the more likely it is we can deploy this initiative.

Either way, this would have allowed for us to have constant supervision of field employees, anywhere in the country.



## **Taking the Next Steps**


Look, if you've now gone through all of this, I'd love to invite you to a free training workshop we've launched as a result of the overwhelmingly positive response we received on this topic.

This training will give you a further look as to what we built and how you yourself can start building basic systems and processes into your own organization.

It's my belief that what stands between you and the ability to exponentially scale your company is removing you as the glue that holds it all together.

People make mistakes, it's just part of who we are as humans.

But if we can build a machine that handles most, if not all, of these processes, we can free up significant time for work that moves the business forward and cut an astronomical amount of expenses in the process.

The moving and installation business is a nightmare, and yet we were able to find a solution.

That said, I know you are just ONE system away from revolutionizing your business forever...
