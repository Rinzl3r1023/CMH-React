## The 12 Week year With brian moran

[Brian P. Moran,](https://www.linkedin.com/in/brianpmoran/) New York Times Best Selling Author, shares great advice and takeaways from his book, [The 12 Week Year](https://amzn.to/3cBTBG7). Alongside his business partner, Michael Lennington, they were able to conceptualize the idea of the book out of the work they were doing with their clients.

[The 12 Week Year](https://amzn.to/3cBTBG7) is a powerful mechanism because it was extracted from what people really need from big companies to solopreneurs. You can experience result much sooner because this guides you to execute consistently on things that matter that moves the needle.

One of the things you will learn is that you have to get rid of the mindset,  *making it up*. You may make it up enough to hit the goal. But think about it, you can go back up and make up the capacity left on the table last week or last month.

These are the things that you’ll learn:

**20:16 -** If you’re a solopreneur, who to seek for perspective.

**24:04 -** Analyzing what the market has been telling

**26:09 -** Patience and persistence in entrepreneurship

**28:03 -** How to create habits to use the 12 week year

**31:01 -** Where does the big motivation come from.

**32:41 -** Determining your true vision
