#### **Episode Synopsis**

In this episode, we talk about how my wife and I were able to take 13 short trips in 2017 and spend no more than $1200 on any of them.

We discuss how you can travel on a budget by just being flexible and using some of the top tools I recommend and personally use. As an entrepreneur, you may be able to find some pretty sweetheart deals just by checking out trips during the low season.

We wrap up talking about why travel is important to keep fresh perspective in your business and how you can use notification tools to let you know when the best deals are available.
