> **I just can't understand how to connect with Apple podcasts. Do you have a video on how to do that?**

Great question, Natalie.

I did not have an episode at that time, and here's an episode for it now.

So let's go ahead and jump into it.

Alright, so when you go to your Libsyn account, the general theme is you want to have what's called an RSS feed.

So go to Destinations. And then you'll grab that Libsyn Classic Feed and copy the link.

Next is, go to Apple Podcast Connect at **[podcastconnect.apple.com](podcastconnect.apple.com)**.

And then, once you are logged in and submit your information, you'll see your uploaded shows.

So you will click the Plus sign and go to New Show. Then make sure to choose to Add Your Show With An RSS Feed.

Paste there the Copied RSS link from Libsyn and hit add.

And you're set. That is it.

## **PRO-TIP**

Quick pro-tip, there is a section under Settings where all of the details for your show will be populated. And this will automatically push to Apple on its own.

So once you set up all your information, description, branding, etc., and you do that RSS feed, it's going to be very easy and benefit your podcast a lot. That's all you have to do.

So it's super, super simple.

Try to get as many reviews as you can subscribe to and people subscribing as you can in those first eight weeks. That's important.

And that's it. You're good to go.
