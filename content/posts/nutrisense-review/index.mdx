## **Continuous Glucose Monitor Device**

This device is called a continuous glucose monitor. You could also hear it called CGM.

It has a needle that sticks into your arm, and it reads your blood sugar constantly.

You will have a running trend that you can notate four or five times a day and throughout the day. You can monitor that data. And you can do it with just a simple app on your phone.

## **Inside the Nutrisense Box**

- Alcohol wipe, to sanitize your arm.
- CGM Sensor.
- CGM Applicator, to apply the CGM Sensor to your arm.
- Bandage, to keep your sensor safe in your arm.

## **How To Put on CGM**

First, you have to apply the Alcohol wipe. Clean the area at the back portion of your arm with the most fat. That’s where you want to apply your CGM sensor.

Next, open the sensor applicator and sensor pack, line them up, stick them together, and push it down, and you can hear it pops.

And then go to the fatty part of the back of your arm. And push to apply the Sensor.

And it’s applied without any pain. It may sting a little bit, but it does not hurt.

Next, is important, that you take off the four outer rims of the adhesive Bandage, but you leave the centerpiece, so it doesn’t pull on the Sensor itself that doesn’t need to be sticking to the Sensor.

And then you stick it on your CGM Sensor.

And you’re all set.

## **Scan to Activate Your Nutrisense Sensor**

Go to the app, select Settings, then Sensor, and Activate New Sensor.

Once a prompt appears, place your phone with the NFC into your arm where the CGM device is. Hold it there for a second.

And you will feel a vibration once the Sensor is scanned.

Then it will be Activation Successful. It’s that simple.

And then you’re going to wait 30 minutes before it shows up.

## **Monitor Your CGM Data**

- Exposure is the area under the glucose curve for two hours following the meal.
- Stability is the measure of how much of a glucose jump you had.
- Recovery is how close your two-hour glucose value is to your pre-meal glucose value.
