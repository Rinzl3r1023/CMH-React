## **Setup Google Analytics**

So let's go ahead, and I'll show you how to install Google Analytics on your website.

> - Go to **analytics.google.com**.
> - Log in to your G Suite account.
> - And then go to the Admin.
> - Go to Tracking Info, and then click Tracking Code.
> - And then you'll see Global Site Tag, and you're going to Copy all of the codes.
> - Go to your WordPress website.
> - And then go to Integration. If you have a theme, look for the Universal Head of where you will embed the codes.
> - And then Paste the codes.
> - Finally, hit Save Changes.

## **Send Test Traffic**

You'll want to notate if you want to see an increase of one person, one active user. If you see zero after you send the test traffic, it means it is not working. It can take up to 30 seconds. So be a little bit patient and may have to refresh it and try it again.

But this will tell you if it is working and if it is installed correctly.
