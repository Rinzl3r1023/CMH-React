## The SECRET to Find & LIVE Your Purpose
With Dov Baron

We are all confronted with something that is always too big. You initially thought that you couldn't do that, but you can.

What is the bias in your head that's actually blocking you that makes it seem impossible? And when you shift back from that to your dragon fire, which is the soul of what it is you're doing, you start to burn away the lies and start seeing the difference. You've got to be willing to ask yourself the difficult questions.

When you say you believe you are running the impossible, here's a mental strategy that Dov uses with his clients: Pick somebody in your mind that seems impossible for you in your business as you grow as an entrepreneur. Ask yourself whatever it is and write it down what you believe is impossible for you. Then ask yourself this question. *What has to be true for that to be true?*

If you have no skills to scale. Could you get skills? If yes, where could you get it from? Start asking these questions, and go five levels. And so what Dov challenged you to do with this is you go wide.

You might get stuck in business, take something that you believe, and start challenging it. And you start training your brain because your brain is neuroplastic reform and change, stop treat changing those synaptic links. So you can start thinking in a different way.

Whatever you believe. It's only true if you make it true. That's not positive thinking, that's the psychology of humanity.

*What has to be true for this to be true?*

Here's a simple scale for anybody: Look at your life and break it into categories— primary relationships, familiarity, health, fitness mentality, emotionality, motivation, etc. Score yourself on one to 10, go through it, and squeeze up.

Then ask two people who know you really well. How would they score you? You've got to be real and vulnerable. Let them score you. And then ask two people who don't know you as well as the first two. You will get a very different answer. If you average out, what is the number?

What is the belief system you have that keeps you at that number? What has to be true for that to be true?

People talk about changing beliefs, but we don't understand that beliefs collectively are called a paradigm. Beliefs are held in clusters in your mind. So you collapse this belief, and another belief pops in instead, so you actually have to go to the root instead. What has to be true for that to be true is a five-level beep that makes you go okay.

**Here's what Dov believes:**

He believes that you did not get your hopes and your dreams by accident. You've got them for very good reason. You found a vehicle, and the vehicle is called entrepreneurship in whatever form it's in. But what is that vehicle for you to fulfill your dragon fire? Whether it's an online course or whatever it is that you're doing, you are actually internally driven by your need to make a difference and to have an impact. So you wouldn't be given that dream without the possibility of having that impact. It's not going to be easy. Let's get rid of this whole easy nonsense. You're going to have to work hard, and it's going to be worth it because it's about fulfilling your soul.
