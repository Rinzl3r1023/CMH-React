But I found myself waking up a lot with Ooler because I was too cold and it doesn’t have that added benefit of even being able to set a certain temperature that lasts throughout the night.

And this does not happen now with Eight Sleep because they have an Autopilot which adjusts the temperature so it helps you maximize the different stages of sleep because it has biometric tools that read what stage of sleep you’re actually in.

I can feel like the cover is a little flimsy. It had a little strap that went around the corner of each side of your bed and it comes off a lot. It didn’t feel like a very durable product.
