## The Problem with Cancel Culture

## with Will Witt

##

[Will Witt](https://www.linkedin.com/in/willwittich/)of PragerU drops by to talk about the problem with Cancel Culture and how it is toxic in our society. He creates online content for PragerU and helps inspire young people worldwide to stand up for the values they believe in.

The thing with conservatism that Will likes the best is the ability to be free from intervention from other people. So you can live out your dreams in whatever way possible in a way that's not hurting anyone else.

And for Will, being someone who dropped out of college and moved to a whole different city, he wants to be able to have the freedom. To be able to speak his mind and put these things out there, and without those kinds of freedoms, he could never have the job that he has now.

So conservatism is definitely the way for him. And he thinks it is the best way to have a free and successful society.
