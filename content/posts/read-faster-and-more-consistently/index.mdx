Tip #1 I'll take any given book and I'll see how many pages it is. Then I figured out what are my next few weeks look like so that I can make a reading plan for myself.

So I pull out my handy dandy calculator. If the book is 158 pages and I divide it into 15 days, it's ten and a half pages a day.  When it looks like it's 200, 300 pages, it feels massive. But if you're going to do it over a month, roughly six to 10 pages a day, that is a lot more doable and more manageable.

**Tip#2 Pick a spot****and the times that you plan to read**. I've played with different areas and places and times. And this is just what works best for me. So please do what works best for you.

The number one place for me is when I wake up, I'm eating breakfast and getting ready for the gym. I knocked my six and ten pages out right then and there. I like to read and let it marinate on my mind while I'm working out at the gym and meditating on that. And the reason being is that part of the benefit of only reading and doing sittings of six to ten pages at a time, not to say it's all going to read all day, and the significant benefit of that is you can retain the information better.

We know that the human brain is better at sprints, not marathons. If we can go in little sprints of 60 to 90-minute windows and take breaks making our brain works a lot better.

Everybody has 10 minutes at breakfast. And if you don't and that's not your situation, then maybe when you send your kids off to school and on the way home, you listen to an audiobook. So whatever time that is,  make it more digestible. .

**Tip #3 Take your book with you.** The benefit of audiobooks in kindle is you always have your digital devices. I like physical copies of books. And the reason being as another little tip, I like to **highlight things.**

Highlight things when you're reading, that will help you retain and memorize things. And think about meditating on like how this actually applies to you and your life or your business.

Take your book everywhere you're going. So, if you're going to be on the plane or you're going to be sitting somewhere. There's your 10-15 minutes right there. So we need to just take advantage of the time that we have.

**Tip#4 Get some kind of noise-canceling headphones.**I'm just more dialed in when I don't hear all other noise in my surrounding. There are a couple of stations that I like on Spotify that are more like instrumental, beat type music, low-fi beats where there are no words in the music and the songs because if I hear the words and it's competing in my brain for that as well, it's a really tough thing for me to be able to do.

**Tip#5 Try to** **meditate.**Try to make meditation an active part of my day, the reason being is that we have all these things that are pulling us noise in directions, and we have way too much beta brainwave state, which is an active brainwave state. And that's not conducive to learning and to being in a flow state.

What I try to do is make sure that I am just as much as I'm active and things are pulling me to be active. I'm also counterbalancing that with some more theta, lower brainwave state calming. I think we underestimate how important that really is for our focus and concentration.

So those noise-canceling headphones help me achieve a 10 to 15 minutes of meditation with these noise-canceling headphones on and you're listening to something that's more of an instrumental type, even classical music has been proven to help people a lot. I think you're reading, you're going to find you're retaining so much more than you would otherwise.

**Tip #6  Stay consistent.**One of the problem is that you are well-intentioned, but you don't have a plan. And then you try to come back to the book, and it feels like, two months later, you have to restart because we forgot a lot of what you read already. There's this huge gap, and you spend the first five pages reading trying to connect the dots.

So, pick a book, have a plan, and then stay consistent. And oftentimes if you want to take a break, plan a buffer between finishing the book. And get like six days of not reading before reading a new book.

We know that 66 days is what it requires on average to build new habits. That's a little bit over two months. If you can just stick with it, read every day, pick your spot, read in that spot every day, wear noise-canceling headphones, and take your book with you. All of these things we've talked about. What you'll find is it's just something you do. You just go into autopilot. We have to understand the benefit of success habits.
