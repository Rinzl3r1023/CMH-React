## **Our Hobbies Define Who We Are and Become**

Look, here's the deal, I get we have a million things going on in life and it's so easy to let your hobbies fall by the wayside. We have companies to run, people to pay and competitors nipping at our heels; even when we sleep.

But here's the thing and this is what I want you to remember when you have these internal struggles about your entrepreneur hobbies...There's a reason Richard Branson is so successful and yet you constantly see him on some new adventure, pushing the limits on some new excursion or enjoying an experience on some private island (he just happens to own).

When you engage in your entrepreneur hobbies, you're not taking anything away, you're investing in YOU. The most important asset to the business.

When you're happy and fulfilled, your business will be energized, full of life and probably thriving.

I remember being a young entrepreneur and telling myself that one day I'll be able to enjoy life again. One day I can resume my hobbies, explore the world, live like a Branson and do the things I saw other successful entrepreneurs doing. And while I'm certainly not in a position to go buy my own private island, I definitely shot on air ball on my false viewpoint that those things had to be a destination and not part of my journey.

You see, our hobbies are hobbies because we feel passionate about those activities. Something inside of us comes alive when we take time to just be present in those moments and enjoy something we are willing to dedicate so much time and energy in doing.

What I later found, through much trial and discovery, is I was doing myself a major disservice by NOT incorporating my hobbies into my busy schedule and it was robbing me of the person I am and diminishing my vibrant personality. I wasn't fully fulfilled and my business started to feel like a job. That's not why we left our 9-5's, right?

We left for freedom! We left for the ability to take an afternoon on a Friday and go fishing. The 9-5 folks are the ones that have to wait to clock-out but we sacrifice way too much to not enjoy the ride.

Business opportunities sometimes come about in the craziest of fashions, as I discuss in this episode. Some of the most lucrative contracts I've ever landed didn't spawn from a business discussion but rather a casual chat about passions and hobbies.

So, here's what I recommend you do.

Go find what you used to do that lifted your spirits and made you feel like time stood still. Go spend that time with the people that made you take your mind off of business for just one brief moment.

I promise you'll come back from those entrepreneur hobbies even more motivated, driven and refreshed than when you left the office to begin with.

Enjoy this journey, you're exactly where you need to be and your path to success is not going to be derailed by a few hours per week engaging in the things that make you happy.

Hope you all enjoy…
