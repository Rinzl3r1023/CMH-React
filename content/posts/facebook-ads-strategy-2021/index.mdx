## **PRO-TIP**

One thing you can do as a quick pro-tip is take that ad once it's running and then create an engagement campaign.

Make sure that when you're setting your campaign objectives, you're specific about what you want.

You don't have to trick the algorithm. If you're going for leads, go for leads. If you're going for purchases, optimize for purchases. If you're going for video views, go for video views.

You can do another campaign, pull in that post ID for that campaign, and run it for engagement.

So what that does is you're going to get a lot of positive engagement, comments, likes, and shares on that ad. The same ad is simultaneously running as a lead optimization campaign.

And that tells Facebook the ad is legitimate and should be shown to more people.
