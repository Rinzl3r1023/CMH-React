## **You're Busy, I'm Busy, It's Still Not an Excuse**

Would your friends and parents recognize you without the ability to see your recent pictures on Facebook or Instagram?

In my case, I live 1,000 miles away from my closest friends and family so I have a bit of an excuse.

But you get my drift.

We enter into the world of entrepreneurship and start to evolve as a person.

You have to in order to succeed, there's no doubt about it.

We begin to find less and less in common with those that aren't on the same life journey we're now on.

It's almost as if you can physically see the gap widening between the two of you like at the end of Fast 7 when Paul Walker veers away into the sunset while Vin Diesel continues along his character's story arc (#TearJerker).

[via GIPHY](https://giphy.com/gifs/paul-walker-vin-diesel-dominic-toretto-s8lohqu0U5ZWE)

Most of us get frustrated with this process, desperately trying to get our loved ones to see our newfound mission in life.

And despite our efforts and unless we come from a family of entrepreneurs, it's likely they just don't get it.

Like, at all!

How is this possible, right!?

It's so obvious!

Work for yourself, build something new and innovative that has the potential to change the world, raise money from investors and experience a successful exit for multiple hundreds of millions of dollars, all before the ripe old age of 30.

Buy yourself a private island and live out your remaining days figuring out what you want to do next.

Ahhh, the liberation from the so-called "rat race."

And yet, all your family and friends want to talk about are the trivial pursuits in life.

What so-and-so is up to, who's dating who, who got married and had a baby recently, etc., etc., etc.

Not the type of dialogue that gets our entrepreneurial juices flowing.

My wife and I recently joined in on a group, get together.

Just a bunch of buddies of mine and their spouses from high school that wanted to catch up, grab some grub and a few drinks.

The conversation largely revolved around kids, school districts, 401K's and some of their most recent rendezvous...all of which I can only surmise contained similar elements of dialogue.

My wife and I departed and thought we were a pair of aliens!

How could they not want to talk about industry disruption and the latest business trends?

In fact, my wife actually has to often times help me transition into those settings so I don't bust in the front door, guns a blazin' with my elevator pitch.

Direct to the point. What's my ask? How can we augment our systems and processes to eliminate waste?

It's actually quite humorous when I imagine what they must think.

A friend of mine's mother overheard one of our phone conversations.

From that point forward she officially calls me "Mr. Intensity."

It's just the way we roll and again what's required in this life we've chosen for ourselves.

## **But It's Also Important to Cool Your Jets Every Once in a While**

I'm not saying ditch your entrepreneur friends and skip the next networking event to go play trivial talk with everyone else.

But I do think there's much to be said about balance and duality.

We're constantly running in Beta mode, solving problems and tackling mountains.

And in that pursuit, we can sometimes lose sight of our own identity and what led us along this path to begin with!

Do you know the last time you laughed and truly meant it?

Do you remember the last time you could just relax and not think about business, 24/7/365?

Because hanging with entrepreneur friends is sometimes all about the next pursuit.

There's often no celebrating the milestone, it's just off to the next challenge.

At least that's the case for my fellow hyper-achieving, Type A folks.

But family and non-entrepreneurial friends have figured out something we often haven't...

Joy in the simplicity of life.

I often envy their ability to just be in the moment, basking in their present company and not having another care in the world.

That's not to say they don't have real pressures, responsibilities and dissatisfaction because that, in itself, is just a part of any human experience.

But as an entrepreneur, I guarantee most of them in relation to you aren't ruminating about the next revenue milestone or marketing campaign.

For most of them, as long as they can pay their bills, put their kids through the best schools and live a relatively healthy life, that's all they need to get up in the morning with optimism to do it all over again, day in and day out.

What a truly beautiful thing.

Because at the end of the day, if we kill ourselves along this journey just to make more money and grow our Instagram following, are we not missing out on what actually makes us human?

Real connection with real people that love us unconditionally and just want to share what little time we have on this planet together enjoying our uniqueness with one another and just forget about the rest of the world and it's issues, even for a brief moment in time?

## **And This is Where the Magic Happens**

When we allow ourselves to take that deep exhale, feel the stress roll off our bodies and let our metaphorical hair down, what we find is our problems are not nearly what we make them out to be.

We depart and on our voyage home we have a feeling that life is going to be alright.

Whatever has been consuming our mental faculties for weeks, if not months, has a viable solution that you are more than capable of solving.

And you, yes you, the highly motivated, driven, successful, busting a$$ entrepreneur, you, are still just the same kid that used to run around at recess and play silly, flirtatious games with your first crush.

The same young adult that remembers the joy in buying your first car and taking it out on your first real date with permission from both of your parents.

And, more importantly, the same vivacious dreamer that had this awe-inspiring idea to venture out into the world and try to provide it with a value the likes the world has never seen.

When we depart from that reality and become the business owner we're required to be, a part of us gets lost in the shuffle and the madness that can and probably will ensue.

Through every hateful email from a customer we become a bit more and more cynical.

Every low balance, bank notification leaves us with a pit in our stomachs large enough to fill the Pacific Ocean.

And every failed initiative leaves us believing maybe we're not enough to succeed in this world.

But you, my friend, are everything you need to be anything you set your sights on.

And despite your family and friend's inability to conduct a board meeting with you, your past persona lives on with each and every one of them.

Who you inherently are gets pulled back out of the coat closet like an old board game.

You begin to laugh about the times you shared together.

Cry about the moments that changed and shaped your life.

And, in those moments, realize that for every stupid and silly negative and/or hateful moment or terrible customer that there's people out there that love the living bejesus out of you just the way you are.

So, next time you find yourself frustrated because no one "gets" what you do, how you make money, IF you make money and what the next big plans are for Q4 of the upcoming year, remind yourself you're on a personal vacation and all of those things will most certainly still be there when you get back.

Keep pushing, my friend…I believe in you
