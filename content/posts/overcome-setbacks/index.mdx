## **Are Setbacks Really a Blessing in Disguise?**

If you're in the middle of a setback right now, and maybe you're even calling it an all-out failure, it probably feels like the world has betrayed you and everything is collapsing around you.

Truth be told, in some of my worst lulls, I've experienced the depths of depression, pondered the point of my own existence and even noticed suicidal thoughts slipping into my subconscious mind.

Why?

Because as hyper-achievers, we can sometimes wrap so much of our identity up in what we do, the mountains we have climbed and are currently climbing as well as the lives we impact and the money we put in the bank.

We sprint in life, we don't walk...We sink so much of our heart and soul into what we do that the impact is far greater when we do slip and fall to smack our face against the metaphorical cement sidewalk.

The aspects that allow us to achieve big things are the same demons that rear their ugly heads when that nightmare word called "failure" shows up in town.

So, what can we do?

From personal experience, I can tell you it felt like I was completely starting over.

As if nothing I had done counted for anything.

The score was 0-0...me against life.

Here are 10 quick tips I recommend for keeping proper perspective and ending up far better off than you currently are in this moment of your perceived failures.

## **1. Use This Time for Reflection**

Hyper-achieving, Type-A's usually spend very little time thinking about what they've accomplished.

More often than not, we rarely even celebrate our achievements before we're off to the next big frontier.

This serves us well and we justify it to our emotional brain by saying things like "it keeps me hungry," or "I don't want to lose my edge."

Our masochistic approach is sometimes what backfires and setbacks are the perfect opportunity to re-embrace just how far you've come in life.

I recommend a heavy dose of meditation for people that fit this description.

A very deliberate and intentional way to force yourself into a state of gratitude and self-awareness.

I also recommend being very detailed about the ways in which you've changed your life and impacted others.

This setback your experiencing is a time out, so to speak.

Bask in it as it's just the blank page between chapters before you're off and running again, writing new legacies.

But before you're quick to turn that page, what did you learn?

How did you grow?

Who did you impact?

Are you closer or further from your goals?

What do you truly want to do next?

The reason I'm challenging you to answer these questions is because we are the types that are usually capable of doing anything we put our minds to.

But what we COULD do doesn't always mean it's what we SHOULD do.

Huge, fundamental difference here.

Just because you can acquire and master the skills to continue along this path you're on doesn't necessarily mean it's the best use of your time and abilities.

I believe these setbacks are nature's way of forcing us into analyzing our next steps.

The sooner you get through these reflection periods, the more efficiently you're working towards getting back up on the saddle and off to the races again.

## **2. Recognize You're Going to be Okay**

When was the last time you saw someone fail at their goals so hard that the seized to exist?

Totally failed so bad they literally died.

Unless your name is Evel Knievel, this probably doesn't apply to you either.

When we fail or experience setbacks, it can and probably will deliver a serious blow.

Trapped inside the emotion of that predicament, we fail to remind ourselves of the most important aspect of all.

ALL great people that do tremendous things in the world fail...and fail often.

I've never met a single person in my life, whom I admire and aspire to be like, that hasn't miserably failed at points in their life.

And I'm talking challenges like losing everything they've ever worked for over decades, having to or barely escaping a personal bankruptcy, experiencing some major health scare that could have claimed their lives.

We live in a world of overexposure and visibility with social media.

We see the Tai Lopez types posting a new video or advice piece every three minutes and just assume there aren't any real challenges behind the scenes.

We isolate ourselves into a state of victimhood.

And in so doing, we believe we're the ONLY person that has ever experienced what we're going through.

But you, too, are going to be just fine.

You will pick yourself back up and accomplish even more than you've done to this point in your life.

In fact, more often than not, the achievements beyond a major setback or failure usually completely DWARF any successes prior to the setback itself.

You're growing so much through this setback and you've learned so much more than you knew prior to this point that you're just on the cusp of explosion and bursting onto the scene.

Something was missing from the equation before and you're about to capture that and finally put all the pieces together.

EVERYONE, and I mean everyone who does something special in the world, has been there.

You, too, will be just fine...relax.

## **3. What Can You Learn?**

What did you miss?

When did you zig when you should have zagged?

Because here's the thing...you're going to get another stab at this.

And the stakes are probably going to be much higher.

You're also going to be able to possess knowledge that not only changes your life but the lives of many others.

I have an ongoing joke with a dear friend of mine that I was an idiot six months ago.

And every time we speak, I tell him what an idiot I was six months ago.

I obviously don't mean it in a malicious sense but in one that we recognize just how much our perceptions, viewpoints and skill sets continue to evolve over time.

I embrace that now...

In fact, I fear the day I won't feel that way about how much I'm learning and growing.

Often times, a setback means there was something we should have learned along the way but missed out on that opportunity.

For me, it was seeking outside help and resources.

Prior to one of my big setbacks, I just thought I could figure it all out on my own.

A true pioneer, I would tell myself.

You see, so many people around me had paralysis by analysis or were what I call habitual learners.

They had spent thousands upon thousands of dollars attending seminars, buying online courses and spending hundreds of hours reading books but had no real, tangible results to show for it.

Meanwhile, I was scaling my first business to multi-millions with no prior entrepreneurial experience and zero outside capital and doing so from my college apartment.

But here's the deal...

That cavalier approach to figuring it out along the way means you're going to experience severe growth pains.

And while we were hitting our revenue goals, it was costing us big time and I was ill-prepared to run an organization of that size.

And that's not to say the answers to my problems were inside a book somewhere.

But a healthy balance between educating myself and working to implement what I was learning from actual experience would have served me well.

This is a staple of my approach now.

I hyper-focus on what I need to learn to master a certain area of my life that I'm currently working on.

In so doing, I go through the growing pains way more efficiently and quickly...not to mention in a way less severe manner.

So, what's your BIG thing?

Maybe you need mentors and don't have them.

Maybe your goals aren't in alignment with who you want to be in the world so you're in the midst of an everlasting internal conflict.

Maybe you really just aren't skilled with finances and so there just never seems to be enough money at the end of the month and it's de-motivating you.

This setback is the perfect opportunity to have an honest and transparent discussion with yourself about where you can improve.

## **4. How Much Can You Grow?**

Pain demands growth...

Diamonds are created under extreme pressure.

Your setback is nothing but a catalyst for growth.

In my major health scare, I never realized just how capable I was.

The level of self-discipline I displayed to eat the foods I needed to recover and stay away from junk, to not drink caffeine (AT ALL) and eliminate alcohol, to be in bed by 9:00 PM sharp. All of this at the same time.

My persistent and tenacious approach on an minute-by-minute, hour-by-hour, day-by-day basis vastly exceeds anything I've ever done in my life.

And that includes any business accolade.

My wife and loved ones were constantly in awe of just how inspired I was to beat this monster that was stripping me of life and energy.

While it certainly robbed me of my enthusiasm for success and financial prosperity, something inside of me was growing.

I became appreciative of life and stopped taking it for granted.

When you wake up for several years and just want to feel normal, you begin to recognize just how important the little things truly are.

I would watch people laugh and enjoy their days while I was down to only five foods I could eat and having to sleep 12-14 hours/day.

No amount of rest, nutrition or motivation could alleviate my issues.

I was trapped inside this prison cell and forced to watch the world around me grow and progress.

I constantly felt sorry for myself.

I blamed and cursed God.

I wondered why this world was so cruel and focused on all the negativity in it.

This went on for months until I started to feel a shift.

My health started lifting back up out of this pit of despair.

And slowly, I saw the after effect.

A person that had not only morphed into one with enhanced skills but a new lease on life and appreciation for even the smallest of things this existence has to offer.

Version 2.0 of me had shed it's former skin and grown to new heights.

But were it not for the tremendous pain, both literally and figuratively, I promise it would not have happened...at least not to that degree and in such expeditious manner.

A new, amazing, advanced version of you lies inside of you.

There's this potential waiting to be unchained.

The struggle of your current setback may just be the fire to ignite that evolution to the ultimate version of who you can ultimately become.

## **5. Focus on One Step At a Time**

This is a hard one for us.

During a setback, we look all the way up to the top of the mountain only to recognize just how far we've fallen or have to climb.

And while I disagree with that assessment, I recognize more than most your perceived reality is all that's real to you at this exact moment in time.

So, how can we re-build motivation and start moving you back in the right direction?

By focusing on one step at a time.

During my health setback, it was an amazing achievement if I felt well enough to walk my trash to the dumpster at my apartment complex.

Seriously...no exaggeration.

That's such a far cry from what I was doing just month's prior in running a multi-million dollar startup.

But again, if that's my paradigm and relative perspective, how am I NOT setting my self up for failure?

This is the new ballgame and I can only play the game right in front of me.

So, how can I go from feeling well enough to take the trash out to also doing the dishes?

And then how can I feel well enough to take 30 minutes/day to work on my online business.

Truth be told, what I found was I've now published almost 150 podcast episodes since that health crisis began.

In other words, you've probably never listened to the healthy version of me if you follow my show...EVER.

I've had three Sharks from Shark Tank on my show, 30+ New York Times Best Selling Authors and Primetime TV Hosts as well as having iTunes briefly rank my show in the Top 5 in the world under Business, Health and Education.

Again, all while literally barely being able to take my trash out.

How?

I played the small game right in front of me...every. single. damn. day...for over two years.

And with a chronic illness there's never any guarantees you'll ever fully recover.

The truth of that statement I just wouldn't allow myself to mentally visit but it's truer than the blueness of the sky.

In the midst of your setback, just put one step in front of the other and I assure you that you're going to eventually reach a point where you see and feel yourself lifting right up out of your challenges.

But sitting around reflecting about what you can't do right now or what you didn't do before is just going to keep you stuck in a mental and emotional purgatory.

ONE. STEP. AT. A TIME.

FOCUS...

## **6. Manage the Space Between Your Ears**

Speaking of the mental battle, let's examine that one a bit further.

You see, we have a tendency of letting the physical world dictate our behavior.

In so doing, we believe the solutions to our problems lie in purely the physical form.

We tell ourselves "I just need to make more money," or "If I could stop procrastinating all the time," but these are often just the manifestations of problems rooted much deeper in our psyche.

However, digging down into the depths of who we are requires vulnerability and the exploration of things that may not provide pleasantries.

But if you don't dig a layer deeper, you're never going to find the gold.

Back to my health example, I spent over one full year focused purely on the physical aspects of my recovery.

Failing to unearth some real issues that had been prevalent for years, I could never fully break through the glass ceiling.

What we discovered was my inner turmoil was causing a physical response.

That response led to overproduction of cortisol and the inability for my body to fully rest and heal itself.

It wasn't until we addressed these underlying mental monsters that I was able to fully turn the corner.

And wow, once we did that, it was like throwing magic fairy dust all over my body.

Within a matter of just weeks, we saw improvements that trumped much of what we had fought for over the previous 8 months.

And while I'm not disputing physical efforts are indeed prudent, it's often never the catalyst for the true change you're seeking.

And that goes for your situation right here and now.

This can be applied to more than just a health scare!

Be willing to get vulnerable and candid with yourself.

Start introducing healthy rituals to discover the ways in which you're feeling.

That mental shift is just one revelation away from changing your entire life.

## **7. Ignore the Outside Noise**

It's so easy to get caught up in the noise when you experience a setback.

We often want to deflect how we're feeling and to just focus on external factors.

It's why many people would prefer to just turn on a "feel good" movie when things are gloomy.

Sometimes the pain is too great or too overwhelming to focus on and so it requires less to focus elsewhere.

In this day and age, this is a path that can only lead to more pain.

Why?

Because we live what I call photo album lives.

You'll never see someone get on social media and present themselves without first making sure it's the absolute best picture they could possibly take of themselves or run through the perfect filter.

We only display our best selves and smiles to the world when behind the curtains of our reality we paint a far different picture.

70% of people in America are living paycheck to paycheck.

Think of that...7 out of 10 people you walk by in public.

And yet we hold ourselves to this standard that's conveyed to us on social media and the world around us.

This, right here, is one of the main reasons it's so hard for people to bounce back from setbacks.

What you don't realize is the guy walking behind you or the gal on your Instagram feed is struggling just as much, if not worse, than you are!

I often do an exercise that vastly helps me in this area.

During my morning meditations, I think about how fortunate I am to have the things I have, live the life I live and pursue the dreams I have for myself.

Truth be told, this is the greatest time in human history to do just that!

Think about it...you're reading this thing crazy thing called a blog and people actually buy programs from or hire me as a coach as a result!

What's more is big companies pay me money just to talk or interview other people!

How crazy is that?

And it could be anyone, anywhere in the world.

When has that ever been the case?

This is totally new territory.

I get to talk to my idols on my podcast.

These people would have previously been untouchable and inaccessible in many ways.

On a more simplistic basis, I have a beautiful wife, an amazing family and live in one of the highest rated cities in the entire world!

There are people out there that are enslaved, fighting for their lives, fearful of tomorrow and starving to death.

And here I am complaining about _____!?

Who the hell do I think I am, anyways?

We flood God's inbox with our meaningless wants and desires while our brothers and sisters around the world just want to see tomorrow.

That exercise makes me forget about Social Media life and put things into perspective.

I am blessed, you are blessed and our problems are minuscule, at best.

## **8. Be Patient**

Speaking of patient, did you know the average Doctor only waits 11 seconds before interrupting a Patient?

11 bloody seconds!

How terrible is that?

And that's not just isolated to physicians.

We are extremely impatient creatures.

We've now been so conditioned to want things and have them right at our disposal.

It's why we've evolved so much as a species but it's also why we're driving ourselves mad.

You see, working through your setbacks is not on your terms...

Not completely, anyways.

I believe in a designed plan for our lives.

I also believe the body knows what it needs and when it needs it.

This reset or pause in your life may be a sign from the body and soul to slow the heck down and just wait a darn second!

But we find ourselves trying too hard to push through the setback and ultimately end up making it harder, if not completely prolonging it.

I'm the world's worst when it comes to this.

And I wouldn't just attribute it to being patient but it's certainly part of it.

You see, I view it as a sign of failure when I can't push through my setbacks.

It's only when I allow nature to run it's course that I'm able to glean the information needed to move forward.

Or, in the case of my health, let the body run it's course.

And this is such a tough balancing act.

Because there's being lazy and milking it and then there's a time to diligently move forward with life.

I can't articulate exactly how to gauge the difference between the two but let's just say you know it when you feel it.

It's a total gut thing.

But let's just be clear here...

In due time, you'll reach the light at the end of the tunnel.

Embrace your setback, move forward in an efficient manner and be patient with yourself and the process.

## **9. Get Excited About a New Future**

This is the fun part...

The best way to overcome your setbacks is to again get excited about new possibilities.

I remember taking time to go back and do the things I enjoyed.

Getting out in nature, having fun, socializing and even little things like working on my car.

All of these things had fallen by the wayside in pursuit of my dreams and aspirations.

But now I could re-engage in some of those activities and start reshaping what I wanted to do with my future.

I could paint a new life that included these things I enjoyed.

If I wanted to live a life where I traveled the world and worked solo from my laptop, I could!

I wanted to move to a new city so we did!

We had put off vacations for years so we went on THIRTEEN in one year!

Now, I recognize not everyone is in position to do that but find what works for YOU!

You can now be whatever you want again.

Or you can completely change your current business.

A soft reboot is what I like to call this phase.

And what you'll find is just how important these little tweaks are for your future.

You're also going to find that you enjoy the new life SO much better than the old one and this setback has now become a total blessing in disguise.

## **10. Trust God**

I was going to just put enough said and sign my name to this one...

But let me elaborate for the sake of diligence.

Believe what you want but believe in something bigger than you.

I literally can not imagine life as an entrepreneur without my faith.

Seriously, like total train wreck.

It's my foundation.

It keeps me grounded.

It also makes me realize that there's a purpose behind all this madness.

Without that, what am I truly doing all of this for?

We don't lose motivation, we just lose sight of what we're fighting for.

God has a plan for you and God does things for you, not to you...I promise.
