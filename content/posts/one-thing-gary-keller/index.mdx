And what he found was that *thinking big actually requires focusing small.* It doesn't mean you have to sacrifice your dreams and aspirations. So Gary realized and this new approach that is focusing small, doing less, is that he started having more success and better results.

**The six lies of success:**

**#1 Everything matters equally**. We tend to write down on our list and to have everything on our list. We don't view them and prioritize them in terms of what is important. And if you can't accomplish it, we fall into a taskmaster mode, and that's what I call it. So, you have to divorce your taskmaster.

**#2 Multitasking.**There's scientific evidence that multitasking doesn't fundamentally work the ways we'd like for it.

**#3 Discipline.** Discipline is overrated, and Gary talks about how you want to focus on building success habits, the habits of successful people, not relying on discipline.

**#4 Willpower's on will-call**. We think we can flex at any time we want. It's a depletable resource, so we have to recognize that.

**#5 A balanced life**. Gary has this amazing concept called *counterbalance,*and we don't want to strip away the essence of entrepreneurship because it does require extremes.

**#6 Big is bad.** Wanting to do something big is somehow bad. It is one of the things that people do think is to focus on what you could achieve.

The number one focusing question that Gary brings up—*what is the one thing such that by doing so will make everything else easier or unnecessary.*
