## **Getting Your Butt Kicked**

Man, did I think I was hot stuff...

I used to play this game called "What They Should Have Done."

It essentially involved me being critical of the way someone designed or ran their business.

But, the reality of the situation is I hadn't the slightest clue of what all was involved with doing anything.

I was just being a punk-kid that just thought I knew everything.

So, when it came time to start my first business, I jumped in with zero hesitation, not even putting in a two weeks notice at my job.

Literally, just sending a resignation email and being done, off to "set the world on fire," or so I thought.

Not six months later and I was not only depressed but broke and doing laundry in my bathtub to cut down on my expenses.

Yea, that kind of broke...

I lost sleep, weight, hair, (sometimes) sanity and nearly everyone that loved or cared about me was rightfully concerned.

But, you know what?

It was in those moments that I transformed from some snot-nosed, know-it-all to a real, polished, mature and effective entrepreneur.

More than that, I became a good man.

I learned the value of sacrifice.

What it meant to bust your literal ass for months without seeing any signs of success but continuing to persevere regardless.

You see, it's my belief that we've largely become entitled as a society.

We graduate college and expect our unique and amazing opportunity to come knocking at our front door.

Soon thereafter, we find ourselves back at mom and dad's house and the only person that comes knocking is the mailman with those gimmicks called student loan bills in the mail.

So, we wait and see what is to happen with our future.

Sure, me, as unique, special and gifted as a am, someone is going to take note of my rising star, right?

The clock keeps ticking, we're now in our mid-20's and still going out partying with our friends on weekends, knowing that big opportunity is just around the corner.

Friends are getting married, buying houses, having kids, etc.

We still just haven't caught our groove...

But it's a different generation, right?

You're not ACTUALLY an adult until you're in your 30's in today's world.

In fact, you're actually doing mom and dad a favor by helping out around the house.

When you land that big job and start adulting for real, there will be no time to spend with them anymore.

And then it happens!

We do finally find that job we've been seeking.

The place that's going to embrace all of our ideas and quickly promote us up the chain of command.

It's only a matter of time before we're running the entire company.

Two months in and we're ready to quit.

This is nothing like they promised!

Surely there was some mistake made.

Maybe the got their candidates mixed up in the interview process.

Now, at this point, one of two things can happen...

Either we stand still and realize this is the world we've inherently adopted for ourselves or we venture back to mom and dad's residence to begin the process all over again.

Is this a dire depiction?

Maybe...

Inaccurate?

Probably not by much, whether we have the gall to call a spade a spade or not.

But here's why it matters AND why I'm talking to you about it, regardless if either of these scenarios exemplify you or not.

It's in those exact opportunities, or ones very similar, when we have a decision to make.

The same decision I had to make.

And whether or not we're right, wrong, or even indifferent about our own objective capabilities, what matters is what we choose to do with that motive and energy.

You, like me, might just say screw it and go do your own thing.

Now, you might find yourself, also like me, on your ass and wondering what they heck you were thinking a few months later.

But I digress.

The point is you took action.

And for every situation in life we aim to improve, it's the simple act of taking the first step that I commend the most.

Why?

Because I guarantee one of two things is going to happen.

You're either going to realize that becoming an entrepreneur is a lifestyle, not a career, and every ounce of your being aligns with being an entrepreneur...

OR...

You gain an entirely new perspective about employment life and maybe that A-Hole boss of yours wasn't so bad, after all.

Hell, he even let you take breaks on Holidays!

Sometimes, you would even get paid time to NOT be there!

You get to go home and forget work at 5:00!?

For entrepreneurs, at least early stage, that is most certainly not the case.

Wait, dude, I thought this blog was about pursuing your dreams?

Am I reading the wrong blog?

Nope, right place and right channel, just bear with me for a second.

## **Remember Where the Magic Happens**

Jeff Bezos was recently named the world's wealthiest human being on the planet.

By like, a long shot.

Do you think this iconic picture would have led you to predict that happening?

Unlikely...

Do you know how uncomfortable he must have been in that moment in time?

I mean, legit, the guy basically had a sign made with spray paint in an office not larger than most college bedrooms.

You see, we romanticize success so much so that we forget to really track where it came from.

We see the glitz, glamour, fortune and fame but we don't hear the raw emotion of doubt, potential failure and fear.

And after 141 podcast episodes, majority of which were done with some of the biggest names in entrepreneurship, I've come to learn that success comes with one helluva difficult journey along the way.

Much of what I went through changed me to my core.

The evolution of a person into one that is barely recognizable.

Because it is, in that moment of our struggles, the point in which we become something more than we ever could have dreamed of in our wildest dreams.

Do you think Bezos expected THIS level of success in that moment in his life?

I bet he would have laughed at the mere suggestion.

And yet he trudged along anyways.

So, while I'm all for celebrating success, I also want to recognize what it took to get there.

## **Hitting Our Crescendo**

Have you figured out where I'm going with this narrative yet?

As in, the reason you absolutely must pursue your dreams in life.

If not, I truly forgive you.

My message probably comes from a different paradigm than your usual ra-ra, motivational piece.

And it's for that very reason that you absolutely have no choice BUT to pursue your dreams.

Because, at the end of the day, what do you honestly have to lose?

What's the absolutely worst that could happen?

Seriously, take a moment and write them down...I'll wait.

Still waiting....

If you're like me, you probably quickly realize the worst that could happen is you waste a little bit of time and money.

Which, by the way, most of us do that every single weekend as it is.

But let me ask you this...

What if you, yes you, were to build the next Amazon?

Scratch that...

What if you were to just make enough money to subsidize your current income but to do so on your own time and as your own boss?

Imagine, just for a minute, if your work inspired someone else, or even thousands, to pursue a similar life.

Or that your creation could help thousands, if not millions, do something better than what's currently provided in the marketplace.

It's at this point I usually have people cut me off and commit to a new future.

Literally, at this very moment.

You see, it's because we way over-hype things in our mind.

We act like suffering is the worst possible life experience.

Just like we graduate from college and expect that big job opportunity to come our way, we've become a nation of expectations.

And in those expectations we lose sight of what makes us human more than nearly everything else on the planet.

The power of struggling for what we believe in.

Seriously, it's even enriched in most of our religious beliefs.

The age-old adage about why God allows bad things to happen.

Now, News Alert, I'm not God...

But what I do know is there's something to this universal plan for all of us.

Assuming you believe in that sort of thing, you can see it wedged throughout all of our society.

Every single hour of every single day, we struggle.

And usually, we spend most of our existence trying to rectify that truth.

That maybe, by some chance, we can avoid that train from arriving at the station.

But it comes, just as predictable as the sun will rise tomorrow.

And that's whether you decide to pursue your dreams or not.

Now, maybe my cynical or morbid view of reality doesn't resonate with you.

Maybe you do want the ra-ra, inspirational speech to appease your discomfort.

I enjoy those speeches just as much as the next guy, I assure you.

But when have any of those inspired action?

No, seriously...

When have you heard a motivational speech about pursuing life's dreams and leaped from your seat to change your life?

My guess is you wouldn't be reading this blog if you had.

So, I want you to try this dose and call me in the morning.

Let's see if this cocktail makes you view life through a different lens.

Because at the end of the day, you ARE unique, you ARE special and you ARE talented enough to warrant those knocks at the front door, seeking out your employment status...

The question I'm forcing you to answer is why the hell you're sitting around waiting for it and if that's truly what you even want or just a way to live out your human experience with as little pain and discomfort as possible.

The conclusion I desperately hope you arrive it is that you aren't immune from these realities and that suffering, if you must, for your own dreams, is not only life's grandest pursuit but the one that promises the absolute best version of you as the light at the end of that tunnel.

So, take some time to think about it...

Again, I'll wait...
