If you’re like my business partner and me, we have a membership program and want a more one-on-one environment of actual coaching.

We had to find something that made sense that was also scalable.

And [Voxer](https://www.voxer.com/) is the app we found and has been using with our newly launched tier.

**It is easy to send quick voice notes, and it’s a seamless walkie-talkie communication back and forth.**

**[Voxer](https://www.voxer.com/) really works well and might also be an option for you. **

So, let’s get into it.

## **Phone Number Privacy**

[Voxer](https://www.voxer.com/) has a similar display to another communication app you’re familiar with.

You got all of your contacts, and it will pull in contacts from your phone and get notified.

People have short usernames, and a significant benefit with Voxer is that you can hide your phone number.

So, if you’re in a business setting with privacy concerns. This is a great option.

Whereas some of the other ones don’t.

That’s, that’s one of the biggest reasons that we opted to go for this one.

## **Thread History**

[Voxer](https://www.voxer.com/) has some limitations on the free service versus the paid. It has to do with the history of your thread.

So, if you have a lengthy conversation and need to refer back to something, you will want to have a pro account.

Because with the free version, your older conversations will be replaced with the new ones.

Which is something you have to take note of.

## **Send Voice Notes**

A bit of a pro tip here, you’re going to want to put a Tap to Talk versus Hold to Talk.

You would not have to hold the orange button the entire time you’re talking.

Something else that is really cool is that the person you’re sending your voice call to can actually listen while you’re saying what you’re saying.

Which is good in the sense that it creates quick feedback and communication. This allows you to have a real-time conversation.

So, in terms of protecting your time and bandwidth as a business owner, this is a fantastic ability.

And another ability is you can also hit Recall, which means you can remove your voice note and send another one.

## **Location Tracker**

There’s a little arrow you will see on the right-hand side of the message, and it will provide additional details as it relates to that specific message and shows their actual location.

Why do they include location on this? I legitimately have no idea.

It makes no sense to me.

It’s defaulted On, and I think it should have defaulted Off.

So that’s something you’d want to take note of and make sure you do.

## **Listening to Voice Notes**

Something else I like is that you can move the voice notes backward and forward.

The other thing I can do is I can set the actual speed. I can set the speed in which how fast I listen. I go up to 4x average speed, which is incredible.

Plus, you can forward your voice message. And you can deliver it outside of the app itself, which is really cool.

So, if I want to take this file and send it somewhere else because they’re not on Voxer, you can do that.

So, you can send it anywhere else. You can embed it anywhere, so far as I can tell. And that’s a really awesome winning feature.

So, regarding Voxer’s abilities, it’s a straightforward app but also packs a lot of punch, which is fantastic.

You can send some video features if you want to send a video note to somebody. Again, I think it works for what it needs to accomplish. I think it works tremendously well.

## **CONS TO CONSIDER**

Some things are a little wonky on the desktop version; as I was trying to pause a voice note, it skips to the next voice note, and it wasn’t working as well as I wanted it to.

On the desktop version, there’s no ability to listen to the playback speed at a faster pace. That would be ideal to have that as a feature.

The audio is not great. So, it really sounds like a walkie-talkie. It sounds crystal clear if you listen to an iPhone voice note. It sounds incredible, even though the same device.

But honestly, other than that, I don’t have any other complaints. I think it’s a fantastic option for your communication tools.
