## **Body Battery Calculation**

The biggest thing that I benefit from as a peak performing, busy, stressed-out entrepreneur is the body battery—this shows you where you are in terms of the amount of juice you have left.

Look at yourself as a battery. How much is left?

## **All Day Stress Level Measurement**

One of the biggest metrics they use is called HRV. And it stands for heart rate variability. This is a measurement of the level of stress that's on your body. Some days you have stressed out days, and you can look with the data points, how did that impact you.

It tells me throughout the day where you were at. Then I can make adjustments, and I can make tweaks and changes throughout my day. And I can see if I made a full recovery.

So that's the biggest difference that I see is that it measures all the time versus taking whatever out 8-10 hour window that you sleep, and then you don't have anything the rest of the day you're just uncaged, and you have no idea what's going on.

But this is an absolute winner as far as the daytime measurement.

## **Movement Reminder**

Let's say you have a more sedentary job like you are sitting a lot. And what it will do is it pings you, and it'll tell me certain things like it's time to get up, stretch, and it's time to get up and move.

So it's really good for you not just for your spine, but just overall health and wellness, it's good for you to stand up, stretch, move around.

## **High-Stress Level Reminder**

If you're getting too stressed, it's going to start zapping your body battery, and it'll let you know you're stressed, and it'll tell you to go through this breathing exercise.

It's like box breathing—inhale, exhale, but holding the top hold at the bottom. It helps you regulate your activities throughout the day.

## Data Storage

The other benefit of this device specifically is that any data you want to glean from it is all right here in the device itself. So some of you require the app, this will continue to be utilized.

I don't go to the dashboard for some time because all the data that I need to just regulate what it is that I'm doing and keep up with my metrics. It's right here on full display. And I can just browse and quickly check it.
