Because now, you could see your clients, you could host with 5000 people all over the world, with your headset on.

So there are endless possibilities and abilities. It removes the geographic boundaries that we have in terms of conducting how we do things.

Imagine if you host a webinar and you invite people. Instead of watching a screen with many distractions, they'll their headset on, joining the webinar of your digital classroom where your avatar is presenting. So that could be huge.

And we'll know more about what we can do inside of Meta based on the OS's limitations.

So, for example, if they say, you can't fly in Meta, then there will be industries that spawn built around how you can travel or teleport or whatever you pay a fee for, just like you would in the real world.

So as it relates to you running your business and marketing your business. You can see people walking down the street in there with their headset on in the Metaverse, and they get a popup notification to join your webinar, or they get an ad that shows up regarding your offer to join inside of your workout class.

## **Marketing Abilities**

There's no way Facebook abandons its algorithm. So you'll still have the same advanced marketing abilities that you'll have to target specific individuals, just with a different mechanism.
