## Insert Video in Email with BombBomb with Ethan Beute

##

[Ethan Beaute,](https://ethanbeute.com/) Chief Evangelist, BombBomb shares the benefits and strategies to maximize your business relationships.

*Should you insert video in email with BombBomb?*

One of the benefits of sending video email is it is meaningful because it's truly personal. And you cannot fake that sincerity because humans have been communicating face to face for millennials.

But a video email is not appropriate for every single message. So you do have to pick your spots. So you're going to do some phone calls or zoom calls when you can get together in person over coffee or lunch or across the conference room table or whatever the case may be. We're going to have some email exchanges depending on the nature of the business.

One thing we do is that in most environments, when someone is watching your video, there is a reply with video button. So they can reply back to you with video, even though they're not above on customer. So that video comes back to you through your own account.
