## **Why This Made the Top of Our List**

Look, this one was a no-brainer as I researched the Top 10 New Year's Resolutions.

With obesity levels and preventable diseases on the rise, it's no wonder we're all making it a goal to be healthier and finally get that beach body we've long admired and desired.

But here's why we're failing, in my estimation.

Our desires are too skin deep...literally.

We deeply care about our physique until we're out for a late night rendezvous with friends and catch the aroma of fresh pizza.

We'd love to sport our bathing suit during pool season and be proud that everyone is looking over to check out all that hard work we've been putting in at the gym until we're stuck in traffic on the way home from a 10-hour workday and haven't had anything but a blueberry muffin for breakfast.

Convenience is king and these QSR/Fast Food chains know it.

They pay crazy money to zoom in close shots of their juicy hamburgers that could literally melt in your mouth.

Entire R&D teams work diligently to figure out the latest trends and what will make you crave more of their product.

And we fall for it, no matter how much we fight it.

You see, they catch us while we're weak.

They've positioned themselves to be there for us, just when we need them.

Why spend 45 minutes to an hour preparing a home cooked meal when for just $4.99 you can eat like a king and feel totally satisfied?

## **Our Values Have to Be DEEPER**

You know how and why I recovered from my health ailment that had unknowingly plagued me for over 20 years and eventually reached a culmination point of change or have this be my demise...Because it was literally going to be my demise!

When my Doctor looked at my lab results and told me I was a likely candidate for cancer by the time I was in my mid-30's at the current pace I was on, stuff got pretty real, FAST.

Aspirations for having the best beach bod or showing off my biceps in my extra medium tee shirt seemed so meaningless in relation to that of possibly dealing with colorectal cancer.

My values immediately changed.

In fact, I felt so miserable as my body slowly started shutting down that all I wanted to do was feel normal again.

My aspirations to be a successful entrepreneur were completely out the window. A far cry from where I had been just months prior.

What I valued were the things many of us take for granted.

The ability to have fun, to laugh at a funny joke, to feel deeply about the people closest in your life and to feel excited about the future.

My endocrine system basically packed it in and went into hibernation.

Both my urinalysis and saliva analysis lab results for iodine showed double ZEROES. Lights are on but nobody's home status.

I could go literally half a month and not have a bowel movement (Gross, sorry). Do you know how much of a lead balloon you feel like when you haven't had relief in over two solid weeks?

So, yea...Being the jacked guy at the pool with washboard abs went right out the window.

I just wanted to roll back the clock and start over again.

Unfortunately, I don't have a fancy DeLorean with time traveling abilities so forward ho we go.

## **My Diet Didn't Change, My Entire Life Did**

I've been a night owl my entire life. Browsing late-night comedies, catching up on the old re-runs of Justice League at 11:00 on Cartoon Network and, in my younger years, doing my best Romeo impression on the phone with that special someone at the time until the wee hours of the morning.

When I got to college, those late-night outings were perfect for bar hopping and enjoying the midnight air.

Fast forward to my post-college life and having now grown a startup from launch to $1.2MM in revenue, stress has become my middle name.

You see, people think growing a business is fun and in many cases people envied my situation as they scratched and clawed to move the needle forward towards their revenue milestones.

But the reality of the situation is you CAN grow too fast if you're not ready for it and don't have the proper resources.

A perpetual game of playing catch up and filling the gaps now encompassed most of my daily activities.

Trying to manage cash flow because we were working with $100+ million companies that had Net 30 Terms (at best) that was only invoiced after we had absorbed a significant amount of cost in actually fulfilling the contracted request.

Overseeing 300+ employees across 32 states, hiring a new face nearly every single day with a billion questions and phone calls landing on my desk by the minute.

You think I was taking time out of my day to eat a healthy, home-cooked meal? HA!

So, let's recap...Bad habits of not getting adequate rest, heavy college drinking, a mountain of stress and bad eating habits.

Not the trifecta for perfect health, I assure you.

Not four years later and I received the diagnosis I described above...that's all it took.

## **Here's What Scares Me And Why It Should Concern You**

We'll get into the conclusion of my story when we cover it in long-form on another episode but here's what scares me...

This is very much the story for many startup entrepreneurs.

We can't NOT do things in extremes.

The people we admire, respect and adore have all had nights they slept under their desk, burned the candle at both ends for years to push through and achieve their goals.

Here's what you don't see...

The guy who did all that and didn't make it.

It's like the basketball star who skipped class in college because he was sure to be the next LeBron James.

Okay, but what if you get derailed by a complete ACL tear and you're never the same player?

I'm not saying don't be all in and committed.

I'm not saying slow your roll and don't push yourself to achieve all that you can.

What I am saying you absolutely must, must, must invest in your health if you want to win the marathon that is entrepreneurship.

Losing weight is the beneficiary of living a healthy lifestyle.

Your success in business is the beneficiary of your hard work to grow as an entrepreneur and a person. That includes taking care of your #1 Asset (YOU!)

## **Entrepreneurs go to Crazy Measures**

Have you listened to an episode of the Tim Ferriss Show lately?

Maybe you watch Tony Robbin's: I'm Not Your Guru

Notice Gary Vaynerchuk lost a significant amount of weight from like 10 years ago?

They all learned what I had to learn.

To go far, you must prepare for the battle.

So, look, I know you've evolved into a deeper person and your goals of being a success in business to make your family proud and leave a lasting legacy have now surpassed your juvenile desires to have nice pecs for the ladies in Panama City, but you have to remember your goals come with strings attached.

What are you willing to do for your dreams to become a reality?

For me, that's more important than the convenience of a quick Wendy's hamburger.

## **So How Do I Do It When Time is So Limited?**

In this episode, we dive into the specifics of how I've structured my day to do just that.

I'm not going to lie, it takes commitment and it takes intention.

I give you all my biggest health hacks as well as everything I do to live a healthy life and still have time to strive for my goals and achieve my dreams.

We also break down some strategies you can immediately deploy so you build momentum and it's not something you reflect back on in two years as just some thing you tried one time that was a total waste of time.

It's my goal in this episode to make it easy for you, to give you options and resources to strengthen your understanding of why it's so important to start making this a real priority in your life.

Entrepreneurship is hard. The hardest thing I've ever done in my life.

The last thing you need is another unnecessary challenge getting in your way and slowing your roll on your way to unbelievable success and prosperity.

Get your values right, invest in yourself by investing in your health AND start noticing just how much more you're able to do and achieve when you reach a point of true peak performance.

When you can roll out of bed and feel fired up about life without your usual 10 cups of coffee to get you through the day.

It's a liberating feeling I long for everyone to experience.

Enjoyed our New Year's Resolution Series to the fullest and encourage each of you to go back and listen to look for trends.

Life is hard, there's no denying that and I'm definitely not here to tell you otherwise.

But with the right team, the right focus and the right intentions with a solid execution plan, what you'll find is a basic blueprint for anything you aim to accomplish.

Here's to living a healthy life and becoming the absolute best version of you.

Cheers!
