The first ten years of Mark's career was corporate. He got hired right out of college into a management training program. He was making great money, and it was a fabulous thing, but he used to get in trouble because of things he would say or do. After that, he had an opportunity to do his first stint, working in small business for somebody else, and that became a breath of fresh air. He started to get a whiff of freedom.

It took Mark quite a while to find his authentic self because he had voices in his head. And he had feared that he had things that were conspiring to prevent him from showing up the way he needed to show up. And that's the personal work. But a large part of that opening that up had to do with who he was surrounded with.

*If you're an entrepreneur, nothing is new.* You can read the books. You can get the how-to, you can go to conferences. You know how to do it and what to do, or why isn't it getting done? And that's where we come back to *habits*as one example of what Mark calls a hidden growth killer.

So two things are required for any change to occur—*a desire and a willingness to change*. And until those two things are in place, regardless of who you are, what you're trying to do. Change is going to happen. The desire and a willingness to change imply that you're going to go do the hard things and walk through the fire to get to the other side.

For years, Mark had the desire, but he was unwilling. He was unwilling to confront the obstacles that he's going to confront to get to where he needed to go. Some of it was shame because he felt like he was hitting so far below his potential. And it wasn't until he was ready that things began to change for him.

Change your surroundings and spare some time to read. Mark recommended the book by **Pat Lencioni's Getting Naked**. And when you finally had the right people around you. You have the desire and the willingness to change.

And so until you're ready to make up your mind, nothing's going to happen anyway. So why not just dispense with all that stuff all the time and energy and whatever and say that you are going to make up your mind to go do it.
