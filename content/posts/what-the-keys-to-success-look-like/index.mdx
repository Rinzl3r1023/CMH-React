**How To Overcome Baggage and Conditioned Issues**

We all have experienced disappointments, failures, and trauma at some point in our lives. As a result, we've made decisions that aren't so smart. So we get disheartened, and we think of ourselves as *"not good enough."*

[Jack](https://www.jackcanfield.com/) shared an excellent example during our interview. Think of an army. They're on a mission where they crawl on a muddy ground under barbed wires. After the mission, they're most likely going to take a shower to get rid of all the mud.

It's an excellent example of letting out what's already in there. Jack says that most people have been pouring in and not letting out the essence of who they are.

Let's talk about E + R = O. **It's Event plus Response equals Outcome.** For instance, your wife leaves you — this is an event — how you respond to that event determines the Outcome you experience.

Most people who don't get what they want will blame everything that they can think of. But why do some people handle the situation way better than others? It's because of how they respond to the event. Again, the response determines the Outcome.

Belief also plays a significant role in our response. Your perception will always validate your beliefs. You're going to see things that prove the credibility of your beliefs.

The question that you have to ask yourself is, *"Are these beliefs creating the life that I want?"* If it doesn't, you have to be willing to accept that your beliefs are not working for you.

If you're familiar with [Aroma Freedom Therapy](https://www.aromafreedom.com/?r_done=1), Jack shared [Benjamin Perkus' book](https://www.aromafreedom.com/?r_done=1), which talks a lot about it.

The book details how a positive feeling can be created from your negative emotions. So when you're holding a negative memory, take an essential oil, rub it in your hands and take about six deep breaths into those feelings.

What happens is the aroma goes by the amygdala into the hippocampus, where you're storing your fearful memory.

Then the positive feeling created will dissolve the emotional negativity associated with it. Within minutes, the impact of those negative memories disappears.

This idea fascinates me because it seems like it can happen at an intentional level that promotes a mental shift for the better.

**Jack's Power 20 In The Morning To Prep For The Day**

Jack usually allocates 20 minutes a day for a specific activity. So he spends 20 minutes of meditation, 20 minutes of high-intensity interval training, and 20 minutes of reading.

Doing the same will help you level-up your productivity and ensure success.
