## **CREATING A FLOWCHART**

Once you're in the [Lucidchart](https://www.lucidchart.com/) editor, you can start with an existing flowchart template from the template library or start with a blank canvas and make a flowchart from scratch.

You can choose any shape on the left-hand side, drag it onto the editor, and drop it in for placement. You can input whatever info you want, including images, animations, and links.

You can then connect the shapes by clicking on the red outlined white dot on any shape and dragging a line out to connect or point to your desired shape.

And something I found was exciting is that if you get to the end of the page, which you can see, there are these tiny dots. And I was like, shoot; we're at the end, let's create a new one. And I could keep going, and it just expands on its own.
