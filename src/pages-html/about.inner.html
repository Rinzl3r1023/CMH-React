01 The origin

I've been building and marketing businesses for 15 years. Long enough to know the difference between something that changes the game and something that just makes noise.

When AI showed up, the whole industry split into two camps. One panicked. The other hyped everything and sold courses about it before they'd tried any of it. Neither felt honest to me.

So I did the boring, unglamorous thing: I took it into a real business — ours — and tested it. Quietly. On real campaigns, real content, real clients, with real money on the line. I kept what worked and threw out the rest.

That's the whole job. I go to the edge, I find the one or two things that actually move the needle, and I bring them back to you in plain language. Because no one should feel left behind just because they're not "a tech person." You were never the problem.

02 What I believe

Four things I'll say to you again and again.

01

You're not behind.

The feeling is real, but the story isn't. You're closer than you think — you just needed someone up ahead.

02

The tool was never the barrier.

It was never about being technical — it was about knowing what actually matters in your marketing. And that part finally got easier.

03

Keeping up isn't chasing everything.

You don't need every tool. You need the few that count — and permission to ignore the rest.

04

AI amplifies your voice.

It never replaces it. The goal is to sound more like you in your marketing, reach more people, and keep the part that's human.

03 Where I coach

I don't coach alone.

I coach alongside my wife and business partner, Kim, inside The Business Lounge. I bring what's next; she brings the how. When you're ready to go deeper, that's the room.
